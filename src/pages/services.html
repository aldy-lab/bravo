Industrial
outstaffing.

We put skilled field teams and engineers on industrial projects and coordinate them on site — so our partners can scale a project up or down without growing their own headcount.

Three layers, one systemL1–L3
  1. L1

    Field teams

    Skilled trades for shipbuilding, offshore, port and manufacturing projects — mobilised as a team, not one CV at a time.

  2. L2

    Engineering & supervision

    Engineers, foremen and quality and safety staff who lead the work and check it.

  3. L3

    Digital tools

    Our own software, such as BravoDoc, keeps project documentation consistent.
