    BravoDoc

    An AI document validator for engineering projects. It checks and compares engineering documents and flags mistakes, missing links and inconsistencies.

    BravoDoc is a working prototype: it reads PDF and Word files, extracts text and technical data, detects missing or conflicting references and shows the results in a simple web dashboard.

    We are looking for partners to pilot it on real project data.
